Sundered Desert — help and documentation

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, email srijat.mukherjee29@gmail.com with your Unity version, render pipeline and a screenshot of the Console. That lets me help faster.

Requirements

  • Unity 6 with the Universal Render Pipeline (URP 17).
  • On your URP asset: Depth Texture and Opaque Texture turned on (for water refraction, depth colour and foam).
  • About 450 MB of disk space for the package, plus about 80 MB for each generated desert.

Quick start

  1. Install the package from Window → Package Manager → My Assets.
  2. Open Tools → Sundered Desert → Setup Wizard.
  3. Click Create New Desert Scene (about a minute), or Add Desert To Open Scene to place it at any position in your own scene.
  4. Optional: Tools → Sundered Desert → Add Explorer, then press Play and walk around with WASD and the mouse (F to fly).

Common questions

The water looks flat or has no foam

Turn on Depth Texture and Opaque Texture on the URP asset your quality level uses (Project Settings → Graphics / Quality). The wizard's environment check shows which one is missing.

Everything is pink

Your project is not using URP. Sundered Desert supports URP only; built-in and HDRP projects are not supported.

Performance is low

Lower Vegetation density and Rock density in the wizard, or reduce the terrain's Tree Distance and Detail Distance. Every prop has three LODs, so distant plants are already simplified.

Can I use it in a commercial game?

Yes. It is sold under the standard Unity Asset Store EULA, which allows use in commercial projects.

How do I query the biome from my scripts?

Use DesertRegion.Find(position), then GetBiome(position) or GetBiomeWeights(position). See README.md in the package for examples.

Was AI used to make this?

Yes, and it is disclosed on the store page. The terrain, textures and models are generated procedurally by code written with the help of an AI assistant. I designed the biomes and oasis, reviewed the results in Unity and tested the package. No image-generation or text-to-3D models were used.
